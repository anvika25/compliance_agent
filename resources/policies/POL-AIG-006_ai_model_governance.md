# AI and Model Governance Standard

## 1. Purpose
Governs the development, validation and use of models and AI systems, including generative AI assistants.

## 2. Inventory
Every model and AI system is registered with an owner, purpose, risk tier and version.

## 3. Validation
Models are validated before first use and when materially changed. Validation covers data, performance, explainability, bias and limitations.

## 4. Monitoring
Performance, drift and failure rates are monitored. Retrieval-augmented assistants must track retrieval success and unsupported-answer rates.

## 5. Use restrictions
AI systems used in compliance are decision-support tools. They must not make final regulatory determinations, close issues, or take external action. Prompts, tool calls and outputs must be logged.
