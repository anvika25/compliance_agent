# Human Review Requirements for AI-Generated Findings

## 1. Purpose
Sets the human oversight required before AI-generated compliance findings are relied on.

## 2. Review requirement
Every AI-generated finding, summary or recommendation must be reviewed by a qualified compliance reviewer before it is shared outside the Compliance function or used to support any action.

## 3. Reviewer responsibilities
The reviewer must check the cited evidence and policy sections, confirm or reject each finding, and record their decision, name and timestamp.

## 4. Prohibited actions
AI systems may not close a compliance issue, change an issue status, make a final regulatory determination, or contact a regulator or customer.

## 5. Labelling
AI outputs must separate facts drawn from source data, model outputs (such as risk scores), and recommendations, and must state when evidence is missing or inconclusive.
