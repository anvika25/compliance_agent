# AML Transaction Monitoring Standard

## 1. Purpose
Defines how the institution monitors customer transactions for money laundering and terrorist financing risk.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all retail and business accounts, all channels (branch, ATM, online, mobile, SWIFT) and all currencies. Amounts are assessed in CAD equivalent.

## 3. Requirements
### 3.1 Monitoring coverage
All completed transactions must be screened by an automated monitoring rule within one business day of posting.
### 3.2 Transaction approval and release
Any transaction of CAD 10,000 or more must be approved by an employee other than the initiator before release. The approval reference must be recorded on the transaction. Self-approval is prohibited. Transactions released without an independent approval are control exceptions.
### 3.3 Structuring
Three or more cash deposits by the same customer, each between 90% and 100% of the CAD 10,000 reporting threshold, within any 7-day period must be referred to AML Investigations within 2 business days.
### 3.4 High-risk jurisdictions
Transactions involving a jurisdiction on the high-risk country list require enhanced due diligence (EDD) and documented sign-off by AML Compliance before or within 1 business day of release.
### 3.5 Dormant accounts
An account with no customer-initiated activity for 12 months is dormant. Any transaction of CAD 5,000 or more on a dormant or closed account requires a reactivation review confirming customer identity and source of funds within 2 business days.

## 4. Roles
The first line (Payments and Retail Operations) operates the controls. Compliance Testing reperforms them monthly.

## 5. Escalation
Alerts not dispositioned within 5 business days are escalated to the Chief AML Officer.
