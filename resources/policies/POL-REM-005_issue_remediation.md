# Compliance Issue Management and Remediation Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Defines how compliance exceptions become issues and how issues are remediated, validated and closed.

## 2. Issue creation
A confirmed exception is logged as an issue with an owner, severity, target remediation date and remediation action.

## 3. Remediation timelines
Target remediation dates by severity: Critical, 30 calendar days; High, 60 days; Medium, 90 days; Low, 180 days. An issue still open after its target date is overdue.

## 4. Closure evidence
An issue may be closed only when all of the following are on file:
(a) the root cause analysis;
(b) evidence that the remediation action was completed;
(c) independent validation by the second line (Compliance Testing) showing the control now operates effectively;
(d) sign-off by the issue owner and the Compliance approver.
Closure without independent validation is not permitted.

## 5. Extensions
A target date may be extended once, with the reason documented and approved by the Chief Compliance Officer.

## 6. Escalation
Critical exceptions must be reported to the control owner and the Chief Compliance Officer within 24 hours of detection. Critical or High issues more than 30 days overdue are escalated to the Risk Committee.
