# Regulatory Reporting Policy

## 1. Purpose
Ensures regulatory reports are complete, accurate, approved and submitted on time.

## 2. Scope
All scheduled returns listed in the regulatory report calendar, including large cash transaction reports, suspicious activity summaries, capital adequacy and liquidity coverage returns.

## 3. Timeliness
Each report must be submitted on or before its due date in the regulatory report calendar. A missing or late report is an exception, and the regulator must be notified within 1 business day of identifying a late filing.

## 4. Approval
Every report requires approval by an authorised approver who did not prepare it. The approval must be recorded before submission.

## 5. Reconciliation
Reported amounts must reconcile to the source system. Variances greater than 1% must be explained and approved before submission. Unexplained variances are exceptions.

## 6. Records
Submitted reports, reconciliations and approvals are kept for 7 years.
